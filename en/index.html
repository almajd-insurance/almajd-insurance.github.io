Frequently asked questions

Is car insurance compulsory?

Yes. Third-party insurance is required to register and renew a vehicle in the UAE.

What is the difference between comprehensive and third-party?

Third-party covers damage you cause to others and their property. Comprehensive also covers your own vehicle, subject to the policy terms. We explain the difference before you choose.

Which documents do I need?

Renewal: registration, licence and ID. Ownership transfer: old registration, licence and ID. Used car (new registration): possession certificate, licence, ID and inspection report if available. New car: customs papers or possession certificate with the purchase invoice, licence and ID.

Can I renew or buy remotely?

Yes. Send the documents on WhatsApp and we handle the rest, with no need to visit the office.

How long does issuance take?

Once documents and payment are complete, issuance usually takes minutes. It may vary by insurer and vehicle.

How is the price calculated?

It depends on the car value, model year, type of cover and driving record. We compare several insurers and show you the best fit.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, with tabby and tamara. Ask about the terms when you request a quote.

Do you cover electric, Chinese and imported cars?

Yes. We have solutions for electric cars, Tesla, Chinese and imported vehicles. Send your car details and we will explain the options.

Do you offer Oman insurance?

Yes. We offer Oman insurance (Orange Card) for all UAE vehicles travelling to Oman, from one week up to one year.

What should I do after an accident?

Follow the instructions of the authorities first, then contact us on WhatsApp and we will guide you and follow up the claim with the insurer.
